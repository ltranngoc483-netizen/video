DESCRIPTION
A masked woman asks: “Am I pretty?” The story of Kuchisake-onna blends unsettling folklore with a real wave of rumor and fear in Japan in 1979.

One origin legend calls her a samurai’s wife, but that story is unverified. This Short distinguishes the legend from the panic, when parents and teachers escorted children and patrolled streets.

The supposed escape rules vary between tellings. “Don’t answer directly” is advice in some versions, not a universal rule or proof that a ghost exists.
