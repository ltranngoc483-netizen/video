DESCRIPTION
Over $9 billion in liabilities. Roughly $12 million in cash. What happened to First Brands?

The US auto-parts group filed for bankruptcy in September 2025. Prosecutors allege fabricated and inflated invoices were used to obtain financing, with some invoices pledged to multiple lenders.

This papercraft explainer follows the alleged financing scheme and the collapse that followed. The fraud claims are allegations, not convictions.
