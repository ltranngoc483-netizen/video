TITLE
What Really Happens When You Scan a QR Code?

ALTERNATIVE TITLE
One QR Scan. What Happens Next?

DESCRIPTION
Your phone spots three finder patterns, reads a grid of data, and decodes what it contains. That could be a web address or payment information.

For payments, scanning is only the start. The app and financial systems must check the details and authorize the transaction. A scan alone does not confirm that money has moved.
