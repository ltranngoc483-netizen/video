The Internet has an underwater highway. 🌊

Undersea fiber-optic cables carry roughly 99% of intercontinental data traffic. Inside, information travels as pulses of light—but fishing gear and ship anchors can damage the links connecting continents.

Discover the infrastructure hidden beneath the waves with THE INVISIBLE WORLD.
