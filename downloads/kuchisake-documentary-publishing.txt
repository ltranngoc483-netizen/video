DESCRIPTION
In 1979, fear of a masked woman changed how communities in Japan protected children. But a published rumor is not proof of a supernatural attacker.

This eleven-minute papercraft documentary follows Kuchisake-onna through four investigations: the Gifu rumor outbreak, the unverified samurai-wife origin, the social networks that carried the story, and the contradictory rules that listeners invented to escape it.

Folklore, historical publication references, interpretation and dramatized reconstructions are labelled separately. No verified victim or supernatural crime is presented. The reported human-imitator incident is treated as an unestablished secondary account.
