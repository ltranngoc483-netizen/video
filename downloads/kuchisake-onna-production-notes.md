## Folklore versus history

The samurai-wife origin is explicitly labelled UNVERIFIED LEGEND. The script says that origin story has not been verified; it does not present a real identified historical victim or crime. The historical discussion concerns the 1979 rumor panic and community escorts, not a confirmed ghost. Newspaper-like sheets are abstract modern illustrations with no fabricated publisher or report, labelled ILLUSTRATED RECONSTRUCTION / NOT ARCHIVAL FOOTAGE. The group of children is safely escorted by adults, with no attack portrayed. The supernatural smile is a cyan seam in cardboard, with no blood or realistic wound.

The supplied brief named Nippon.com and USC Digital Folklore Archives as research leads. No accessible full source URLs were supplied and those articles were not independently opened in this production. Do not treat the generated sheets as source documents. The ending is qualified as 'Some versions say: don't answer directly', replacing the supplied universal 'Her rule? Never answer directly' to match the user's own folklore audit that variants differ. The recommended title refers to legend and the 1979 panic rather than claiming a definitively verified true human origin.

## Project

`npm ci` in a fresh checkout, then `npm run dev` on port3014. Main composition KuchisakeShort; individual Encounter, Origin, Panic and Funnel compositions are editable too. Five Stills export the four complete labelled hero frames and the portrait thumbnail, all 1080×1920. Original images and clean plates are in public. DejaVu font license is in public/licenses.

Render the main composition muted to out/kuchisake-native.mp4; run scripts/finalize.py to mux audio. scripts/validate.py needs numpy and soundfile. scripts/inspect.cjs and inspect-refine.cjs use an environment-specific Chromium wrapper path; substitute an installed Chrome path elsewhere. scripts/voice.py additionally needs Kokoro ONNX dependencies and external model files, which are not bundled. Generated WAV narration is included so model downloads are not needed to edit or render the finished project.
