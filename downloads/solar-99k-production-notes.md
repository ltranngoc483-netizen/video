## Evidence and energy calculation

All hardware and contents are a reconstructed papercraft teaching model, not authenticated product photography or teardown of the specific99,000đ item. The empty case, cell and ballast visual elements are assembled as a teaching diorama. They do not establish that this item, a particular seller or every cheap solar bank contains ballast or false-capacity cells. Sand/weight labels are procedural reconstruction, not a measured finding.

50,000mAh =50Ah; at a nominal3.7V,50Ah ×3.7V =185Wh **if the advertised capacity is true**. This is not the guaranteed energy delivered through a USB port: conversion and other losses matter. Voltage qualification remains onscreen. The large battery block is an abstract energy icon, not a claim about actual shape, chemistry, size or scale.

Small integrated solar panels may recharge large stores very slowly. No specific panel wattage, weather/irradiance, charge time, actual capacity or measured thermal threshold is asserted here. The charging line is conceptual and has no numerical rate. Heat can increase risk for unsafe batteries; the red warning is conditional, not a statement of inevitable fire or explosion. The small camping fire in the opening artwork is a separate campsite prop, not a depicted battery fire.
