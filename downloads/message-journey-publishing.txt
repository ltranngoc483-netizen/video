DESCRIPTION
You tap Send — but your words don't float straight to another phone.

Follow a message from digital data to encryption, routers and fiber-optic cables, then to the recipient's screen — visualized as a handcrafted paper world in 30 seconds.

End-to-end encryption protects message contents when supported and properly implemented. Not every app or SMS uses it, and an individual message does not necessarily cross an ocean.

Follow THE INVISIBLE WORLD to understand what you never see.
